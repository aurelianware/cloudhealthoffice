## What Requires a Paid Commercial License

A commercial license is required when Cloud Health Office is used in a
**production environment** — meaning any system that:

- Processes **real member or patient data** (PHI/PII)
- Supports **live claims adjudication, payment, or billing** operations
- Generates **revenue** directly or indirectly
- Is used by **end users** (members, providers, plan staff) in a production capacity
- Runs as part of a **commercial SaaS or managed service** offering

### Typical Healthcare Production Examples

| Scenario | License Required? |
|----------|-------------------|
| Local dev with synthetic claims | No |

## FAQ

### Can I use this in development for free?

**Yes.** Development, testing, and evaluation use is free with no restrictions.
Clone the repo, run `docker compose up`, and build whatever you need.

### Can I run this in a sandbox?

**Yes.** Sandbox and demo environments with synthetic or test data are free.
This includes internal demos, POCs, and pre-sales environments.

### Do I need a paid license for production?

**Yes.** Any environment processing real member data, live claims, or
supporting production operations requires a commercial license.

### What about evaluation with synthetic data?

**Free.** You can evaluate the full platform with synthetic data for as long
as you need. There is no trial expiration.
