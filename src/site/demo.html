Texas Medicaid on Cloud Health Office.

A health plan keeps its core system. Cloud Health Office takes the core's daily CPCDS file and adds CMS-0057-F APIs, a second opinion on every claim, and the Texas prior authorization clocks. Two minutes, captioned, no sound needed.

Book a live walkthrough See it on your own claims

What the video shows

From the daily file drop to the Texas clocks.

  1. The daily file drop. Members and coverage load first, then medical and pharmacy claims and UM authorizations, with counts for each file.
  2. A STAR member. Loaded from the drop, with coverage mapped from the core's plan code.
  3. The member's claims, as the core paid them. The same records serve Patient Access and Provider Access as CARIN Blue Button EOBs.
  4. One claim, line by line, exactly as the core adjudicated it.
  5. Shadow adjudication. Cloud Health Office re-adjudicates every loaded claim against the plan's own fee schedule and compares its answer with the core's.
  6. The finding. The core still pays therapeutic exercise (97110) at last year's rate. Cloud Health Office shows the difference by procedure code and by provider.
  7. What it could not decide, listed with what to load next. A claims team works from the discrepancy file. The core is never changed.
  8. Prior authorization on the Texas clocks. Tex. Gov't Code ch. 540 alongside the federal timeframes. The earlier deadline wins.
  9. Poststabilization care for a hospitalized member, due one hour after receipt under §540.0302(b)(3).

No core connector and no change to the core: the plan sends files it already produces. Read how a trial on your own claims works, or see the nightly Inferno results.
