One shadow-adjudication finding, traced to the rate.

Cloud Health Office re-adjudicates the core's claims beside it and compares the answers. This video follows one seeded finding from the report down to the fee schedule line. Under two minutes, captioned, no sound needed.

What the video shows

From the report to the fee schedule.

  1. Last night's file drop. The core's claim file came in, and Cloud Health Office re-adjudicated every claim against the plan's fee schedule and compared the answers.
  2. Every pricing difference is on one code, therapeutic exercise (97110), billed by one therapy provider.
  3. Claim by claim: what the core paid, what Cloud Health Office would have paid, and why they differ.
  4. The discrepancy CSV a claims team works from: one row per differing line.
  5. One claim as the core paid it: 2 units of 97110, allowed $46.90. That is $23.45 a unit, last year's rate.
  6. The plan's 2026 fee schedule: $21.30 a unit. Cloud Health Office allows $42.60 for 2 units, so the core is $4.30 over on this line. The core is never changed.

The demo plan, its claims and the 97110 rate change are seeded for the demonstration. See the full Texas Medicaid walkthrough, or read how claims repricing and a trial on your own claims work.
