For health plan leaders

Modern Core Administration, Without a Risky Replacement.

Cloud Health Office is administration software for health plans. It runs beside the core system you already have, in a tenant your plan controls, and takes on more of the work only when the results on your own claims support it.

This page is the short version for CIOs, COOs, CFOs and compliance leaders. The technical detail lives in the developer hub and the evidence library.

The problem

Most Plans Run on Core Systems That Are Hard to Change.

Decades-old cores: QNXT, Facets and similar systems were designed before cloud APIs, and every new mandate lands on them.

Work you can’t keep: plans pay for implementation and integration work that often stays with the vendor if they leave.

Rules that keep coming: CMS-0057-F is a permanent operating requirement, and CMS continues to add interoperability and prior authorization rules.

The replacement trap: a full core swap is a multi-year, high-risk program that few plans can take on.

What changes for your plan

Modernize One Function at a Time, on Your Schedule.

Less dependence on a single vendor: Cloud Health Office is source-available and runs in your plan’s own cloud, so the work you pay for is built on infrastructure you keep.

Compliance that keeps up: the CMS-0057-F APIs are built in, not bolted on, and the platform is designed to support new rules as CMS issues them.

Data you own, ready for AI: member, claims and authorization data stay in your tenant, under your keys, where your teams and partners can use it.

Works with what you already bought: Cloud Health Office is designed to work alongside your core, clearinghouses, utilization management and interoperability vendors.

Cloud Health Office is designed to work alongside the systems health plans already depend on. Our goal is not to replace every system; it is to make the overall payer technology environment easier to modernize.

How it starts

Prove It on Your Own Claims First.

  1. Send the files your core already produces
  2. Cloud Health Office re-adjudicates them beside your core
  3. Review a claim-by-claim comparison
  4. Decide what, if anything, moves next

Your core keeps paying claims, and nothing a member or provider sees changes. Each step after that requires agreed acceptance criteria, reconciliation and rollback planning.

What we can show you today

Evidence You Can Check, With the Limits Stated.

One million synthetic claims processed end to end in the Million Claim Challenge, with published artifacts. This is local engineering evidence, not a production-cloud capacity claim.

The CMS-0057-F APIs are tested every night with the ONC Inferno test kits, and the results are published. Passing Inferno is not CMS certification or a legal determination of compliance.

The source code is available for your security team to read, under BSL 1.1. Production use requires a commercial license.
