Product Line Context

Platform Engagement — AR in Layer 3, Available in Layer 2

This dashboard demonstrates Cloud Health Office (CHO)'s Accounts Receivable (AR) management capability — period balances, aging buckets, and reconciliation views. AR is core finance plumbing: CHO ships it natively in Layer 3 — Full CAPS Platform (cloud-native end-to-end claims administration) and makes it available as a domain replacement in Layer 2 — Progressive Modernization for customers strangler-figging away from legacy core.

The data infrastructure underneath this UI — payment-cycle data, aging analysis, reconciliation pipelines — also reflects CHO's Managed Data Services posture: payers who want operational data services without full Platform Engagement can subscribe to the underlying ingestion and normalization layers as they productize.
