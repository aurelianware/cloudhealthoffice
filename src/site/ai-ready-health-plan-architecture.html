Architecture / AI readiness

AI-Ready Health Plans Need More Than AI Features.

The durable advantage is an architecture your team can understand, govern and evolve.

Accessible operational data and API-driven workflows provide the foundation. Provenance, auditable decisions and transparent policy make results reviewable. Security, privacy, human review, model evaluation and operational monitoring determine whether a use case is suitable.

Implementation Is a Starting Point for Evaluation.

Implemented

Scoped advisory and workflow paths

Code includes Azure OpenAI EDI rejection suggestions and an Anthropic advisory claims-examiner path. They assist review; autonomous adjudication and clinical decision-making are not validated outcomes.

PAS request/decision persistence and service-line outcomes, CRD uncertainty handling, durable DTR creation/read-back, SMART launch context and approval withdrawal, receipt-based deadlines, CMS metrics and Mongo authorization concurrency are implemented within the ledger's documented scope.

Integration Required

Your data, policies and operating model

Payer data adapters, model credentials, environment configuration, integration contracts and policy mapping require deployment-specific work. Default FHIR data-provider bindings are mocks. External AI data flows require security and privacy review, human approval boundaries and representative model evaluation.

Planned or Unverified

Keep the limits visible

Full standards conformance, production adoption, operational outcomes and autonomous decision quality remain unverified. Bulk export is incomplete; DTR package/prepopulation is limited. The committed fraud-model asset is a placeholder, with no demonstrated detection effectiveness.
