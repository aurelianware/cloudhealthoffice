Start With Compliance. Expand From There.

Most health plans follow a phased approach. Your PMPM covers the full platform from day one — activate modules as you’re ready.

1

CMS-0057-F Compliance (weeks)

Deploy FHIR R4 APIs alongside your existing core admin. Your claims and enrollment continue running where they are. CHO handles the API layer, EDI-to-FHIR transformation, and compliance reporting.

2

EDI Modernization (months)

Route clearinghouse EDI through CHO for multi-clearinghouse failover, real-time 276/277 status, and medical attachment (275/RFAI) workflows. Claims still adjudicate in your core system.

3

Workload Migration (your timeline)

Begin shifting adjudication workloads — claims scrubbing, benefit calculation, NCCI edits, fee schedule pricing, COB — into CHO’s engines. Migrate service by service, not all-or-nothing. Keep your core admin as long as you need it.

Frequently Asked Questions

How is “member” counted for billing?
We count unique enrolled members with active coverage in your system as of the first of each month. This is the same member count you report to CMS or your state regulator. Dependents and subscribers are both counted. We pull the count from your enrollment data — no manual reporting required.
Is there a minimum commitment?
Growth and Scale plans are month-to-month with no minimum term. Cancel anytime. Enterprise plans offer annual or multi-year terms with additional PMPM discounts. All plans include a 30-day free trial.
What if my membership fluctuates seasonally?
Your bill adjusts automatically each month based on your enrolled member count. Medicaid MCOs with seasonal redetermination cycles or Medicare Advantage plans with AEP enrollment spikes only pay for the members they have that month. No true-ups, no overages.
Are there any transaction limits or overage charges?
No. Every tier includes unlimited EDI transactions, unlimited FHIR API calls, and unlimited claims processing. Your PMPM covers everything. We don’t charge per transaction, per claim, or per API call.
Does this replace our core admin system?
It can, but most health plans start by deploying Cloud Health Office alongside their existing core admin (QNXT, FACETS, HealthEdge, etc.) for CMS-0057-F compliance and EDI modernization. Every module is designed to either augment or replace the corresponding function in your core system — you choose which workloads to migrate and when. Some plans use CHO for compliance and EDI only; others progressively shift claims adjudication workloads over time.
Is a BAA included?
Yes. Every tier includes a signed Business Associate Agreement.
Can I evaluate the platform before committing?
Two ways. First, every paid plan includes a 30-day free trial with managed hosting. Second, the full source code is available on GitHub under BSL 1.1 — you can deploy it in your own non-production environment for evaluation at no cost.
