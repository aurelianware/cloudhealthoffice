CMS-0057-F prior authorization, end to end.

One MRI request, from the moment it is ordered to the metrics a plan posts publicly. Two minutes, captioned, no sound needed.

Book a live walkthrough Check your CMS-0057-F readiness

What the video shows

From the order to the public metrics.

  1. The plan's answer at order time. The doctor signs an MRI order and the EHR asks the plan's CRD service what it needs. The answer: documentation, on the plan's MRI form.
  2. The plan's documentation form. The office opens that form (Da Vinci DTR) in the plan's provider portal and finds the member. EHRs get the same form through the DTR API.
  3. The office answers the plan's questions. Answers are typed, never pulled from a chart, and saved as a FHIR QuestionnaireResponse.
  4. The request itself: the ordering provider, the MRI, the planned date and the diagnoses.
  5. Sent as a Da Vinci PAS request. The plan answers at once: received, documentation attached, pended for clinical review.
  6. The decision clock starts at receipt. For a Texas Medicaid member the Texas clock (3 business days) and the federal one (7 days) both run, and the earlier deadline applies.
  7. The documentation travels with the request, so the reviewer sees exactly what the office sent.
  8. Every open request on its clock, with the ones at risk and overdue flagged before the deadline passes.
  9. The public metrics. The prior authorization metrics CMS-0057-F requires plans to post publicly each year, from the same records.
