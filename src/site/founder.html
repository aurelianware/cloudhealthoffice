Why I built Cloud Health Office

I've spent my whole career in payer core administration — building QNXT across QCSI, TriZetto, and Cognizant, and architecting systems inside a health plan at Molina Healthcare. Everywhere I went, the pattern was the same: a capable platform that predates the regulations now being written for it, an urgent compliance deadline, and a gap in the middle that gets filled with consulting hours instead of software. I kept solving the same problems — eligibility, claims, prior authorization, interoperability — one engagement at a time.

Cloud Health Office is those solutions, made durable. It's the compliance and modernization layer for legacy payer systems — meet CMS-0057-F without replacing QNXT, Facets, or HealthEdge — and it's built the way I run an implementation: evidence first, no hand-waving, every claim inspectable. That's why the site leads with a million-claim benchmark you can audit instead of a marketing number you have to trust.

“The people who understand payer systems deeply are rarely the people building the software for them. Cloud Health Office closes that gap — the platform is built by the practitioner, with the evidence in the open.”
