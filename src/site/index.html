CMS-0057-F

Why CMS-0057-F Changes Everything

The Making Care Primary (MCP) model under CMS-0057-F accelerates ACO accountability for total cost of care, requiring ACOs and primary care practices to demonstrate real-time care coordination and accurate attribution well before the compliance window closes. Health systems that wait until 2026 will spend the following year firefighting, not transforming.

The organizations that engage now — validating workflows, building attribution logic, and proving interoperability — will enter the compliance window with a working system, not a procurement.
