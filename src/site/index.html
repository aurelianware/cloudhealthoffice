Compliance still matters. Evidence makes it credible.

CMS-0057-F raises the bar for payer interoperability, prior authorization APIs, payer-to-payer exchange, and operational auditability. Teams that wait until the compliance window is fully open will spend the following year reconciling vendors, interfaces, and evidence after the fact.

The organizations that engage now — validating workflows, building attribution logic, proving interoperability, and testing real adjudication behavior — will enter the compliance window with evidence, not only vendor promises.
