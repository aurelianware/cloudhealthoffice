# Code of Conduct

CloudHealthOffice contributors are expected to keep discussion professional,
welcoming, and focused on building a trustworthy healthcare platform.

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that helps the community:

- Be respectful and specific in technical disagreement.
- Assume good intent while still asking for evidence.
- Keep reviews focused on correctness, maintainability, safety, and user impact.
- Welcome healthcare-domain questions from engineers and engineering questions
  from domain experts.
- Protect privacy: do not post PHI, secrets, customer data, or private
  operational details.

Examples of unacceptable behavior:

- Harassment, insults, threats, or discriminatory language.
- Publishing another person's private information.
- Sharing PHI, credentials, or customer-sensitive material.
- Repeatedly derailing technical conversations.
- Misrepresenting unsupported or planned behavior as implemented.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide, or remove comments; close issues; request changes to
pull requests; or restrict participation when behavior harms the project or its
participants.

To report a conduct concern, contact the maintainers privately. For security
issues, use [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
