# Episode 016 article illustration prompts

These three illustrations were generated as wide, text-free technical editorial visuals. They
share a near-black navy canvas, cyan/teal ingestion paths, emerald terminal outcomes, restrained
amber buffering, and an isometric enterprise-architecture style. Text stays in the article's alt
attributes and captions so the technical meaning remains accessible and editable.

## Asynchronous Service Bus architecture

Visualize a large field of structured healthcare claim records converging into an API intake,
crossing a durable message-bus channel, fanning out to three parallel Kubernetes workers, and
writing terminal outcomes plus lifecycle evidence to durable stores. Keep the flow unambiguously
left to right. No embedded text, logos, people, medical symbols, or vendor trademarks.

## Observation window versus eventual completion

Visualize many claim tokens traveling toward terminal storage across a fixed time boundary.
Nearly all complete before the boundary; a very small amber tail crosses afterward and continues
into the same green terminal store. Run an unbroken audit trail beneath the timeline. Show delay,
not loss or dead-letter failure. No embedded text, logos, or warning symbols.
