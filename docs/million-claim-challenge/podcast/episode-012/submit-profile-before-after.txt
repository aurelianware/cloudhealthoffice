## Interpretation

Every one of the four Mongo-touching operations (claimInsert, idempotencyCheck,
versionQuery, insert) showed the same before-fix shape: a fast median but a P95/P99
spiking 4-8x higher. That shape is the signature of resource contention -- requests
queueing for a scarce resource -- not a cost inherent to any single operation. No
single hop dominated the pre-fix average; claimInsert (39.37ms) and eventPublish
(45.65ms) were roughly equal, and eventPublish's three internal round-trips split
its cost almost evenly (11.67-13.68ms each). Fixing the shared resource (MongoDB's
CPU limit) improved all four hops by roughly the same 5-7x factor simultaneously --
further evidence they shared one root cause rather than each having their own.

P99 for claimInsert/eventPublish remained somewhat elevated post-fix (123.91ms,
28.96ms) relative to their own averages, suggesting a smaller residual tail --
not chased further in this episode.
