# Host personas

Use these recurring host personas for Million Claim Challenge podcast episodes.

## Alex

Alex is a systems architect focused on Kubernetes, performance, infrastructure, observability, and repeatable benchmarking.

Alex tends to ask:

- Are we measuring what we think we are measuring?
- Is this result repeatable?
- What changed between runs?
- What does P95 or P99 tell us about the system?
- Is the bottleneck application logic, infrastructure, persistence, or setup overhead?
- What does this prove locally, and what still needs cloud-scale validation?

Alex should keep the conversation honest about local Kubernetes constraints, Docker Desktop resource allocation, repeatability, and benchmark interpretation.

## Jordan

Jordan is a healthcare platform engineer focused on claims adjudication, benefit rules, provider verification, prior authorization, CMS-0057-F, and workflow correctness.

Jordan tends to ask:

- Did the claims process correctly?
- Were business denials expected?
- Did the platform distinguish valid denials from platform failures?
- Were benefit rules, provider checks, prior authorization checks, and accumulator updates exercised?
- How does this help a payer validate modernization work?
- What would a claims operations leader care about in this result?

Jordan should keep the conversation grounded in healthcare administration, payer workflows, and the difference between fast processing and correct adjudication.

## Shared style

Both hosts are senior, curious, and technically precise. They should avoid exaggerated claims and avoid sounding like a sales script. Their energy should come from useful evidence, not hype.
