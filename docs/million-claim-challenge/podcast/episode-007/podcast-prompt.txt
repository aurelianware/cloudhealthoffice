# Episode 007 podcast prompt

Create a 10-12 minute two-host engineering podcast episode for Cloud Health Office's Million Claim Challenge series.

Use the recurring host style from:

- `../host-personas.md`
- `../intro-script.md`
- `../outro-script.md`

## Episode title

From Benchmark Logs to an Operator Console

## Core thesis

The Million Claim Challenge became more useful when benchmark evidence moved from terminal logs and markdown tables into an operator-facing Mass Adjudication console.

## Source material to use

- `article.txt`
- `benchmark-results.txt`
- `../../pend-validation.md`
- `../../../POSITIONING.md`

## Required discussion points

The hosts should explain:

1. Part 6 made scoring honest; Part 7 makes the evidence inspectable.
2. Why a dashboard should preserve nuance instead of compressing a claims benchmark into one green number.
3. What the Mass Adjudication console shows: claims/sec, P95/P99, workflow checks, unsupported, mismatches, platform failures, and payment delta.
4. Why human-readable MCC claim IDs matter for operator review.
5. Why Claims Management and Mass Adjudication are intentionally different views.
6. How PR #874 enabled validation-status filtering for unsupported and mismatched claim rows.
7. How the console exposed a sampling bug: old samples could report mismatches in the summary but omit the mismatched rows.
8. How PR #875 made claim-result samples evidence-first.
9. What the fresh 5K dashboard evidence run proved.
10. Why 100K should come after live progress telemetry, false-pend sweep, payment-amount scoring, and a post-PR-875 50K rerun.

## Exact fresh 5K numbers to include

- Claims processed: 5,000/5,000
- Paid/adjudicated: 3,852
- Pended: 46
- Business denials: 1,102
- Platform failures: 0
- Observation timeouts: 0
- Workflow checks: 577/650 matched
- Workflow mismatches: 0
- Unsupported: 73
- Throughput: 37.90 claims/sec
- P95 latency: 384 ms
- P99 latency: 466 ms
- Avg payment delta: $66.90, diagnostic only

## Important caveats

The hosts must say that:

- this was a local Docker Desktop Kubernetes run, not a production cloud benchmark
- the fresh 5K run was a dashboard evidence proof, not a new scale headline
- the Part 6 50K result remains the larger published correctness result
- the local demo tenant was long-lived, not freshly reset
- payment delta remains diagnostic and is not yet amount-level pass/fail scoring
- pended observation is still one-directional and does not yet include a false-pend sweep
- live in-progress telemetry is not complete yet; the console watches for newly published runs and displays completed summaries
- unsupported scenarios are not wins; they are reviewable work items

## Tone

Keep the episode candid and practical. The strongest story is that the portal found an evidence problem, and the benchmark got better because the team fixed it.

Avoid overstating the dashboard as production operations. It is a local evidence console moving in the right direction.

## Suggested episode arc

1. Recap Part 6: honest edge-case scoring.
2. Explain the new problem: logs are not an operator console.
3. Walk through the Mass Adjudication dashboard.
4. Explain the evidence-sampling bug.
5. Explain PR #875's evidence-first fix.
6. Walk through the fresh 5K run.
7. Name the remaining gaps: live telemetry, payment scoring, false-pend sweep.
8. Close with why this makes the 100K milestone more meaningful.
