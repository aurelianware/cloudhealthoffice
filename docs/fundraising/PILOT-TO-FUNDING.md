# Cloud Health Office - Pilot-to-Funding Pipeline Strategy

**Connecting Pilot Program Success to Fundraising Momentum**

---

## Executive Summary

This strategy document outlines how Cloud Health Office will leverage its pilot program to generate the traction metrics, customer references, and revenue commitments needed to successfully raise a $2M seed round. The pilot program is not just a sales tool—it's the foundation of our fundraising narrative.

Cloud Health Office (CHO) operates four product lines: **Public Tools**, **Transactional Services**, **Managed Data Services**, and **Platform Engagement** (payer-scale PMPM across Layer 1 — Compliance Accelerator, Layer 2 — Progressive Modernization, Layer 3 — Full CAPS Platform). The pilot program described below is anchored in Platform Engagement Layer 1 — that's the line where the pilot-to-paid-deployment unit of progress lives. Recurring-revenue product lines (Transactional Services, Managed Data Services) follow a different self-serve onboarding motion and produce traction signal independently. Canonical breakdown: [POSITIONING.md](../POSITIONING.md).

## 4-Month Pilot-to-Funding Timeline

### Month 1: Launch 5 Pilot Programs

**Week 1-2: Pilot Kickoff**
- [ ] Finalize 5 pilot customer agreements
- [ ] Complete discovery calls with all pilots
- [ ] Deploy Azure environments
- [ ] Assign success managers

**Week 3-4: Initial Deployment**
- [ ] Complete infrastructure deployment for all pilots
- [ ] Begin workflow configuration
- [ ] Establish weekly check-in cadence
- [ ] Document early wins and challenges

**Deliverables:**
- 5 active pilot deployments
- Documented implementation timelines
- Initial customer feedback

**Fundraising Value:**
> "We've deployed 5 pilot customers in [X] days, demonstrating our implementation speed."

---

### Month 2: Midpoint Check-ins & Case Study Drafts

**Week 5-6: Production Testing**
- [ ] Complete transaction processing validation
- [ ] Run compliance audits
- [ ] Gather quantitative metrics
- [ ] Conduct midpoint NPS surveys

**Week 7-8: Case Study Development**
- [ ] Interview pilot stakeholders
- [ ] Document ROI metrics
- [ ] Draft case study content
- [ ] Request reference permissions

**Deliverables:**
- Midpoint check-in reports (5)
- NPS scores from all pilots
- 2-3 draft case studies
- Reference customer confirmations

**Fundraising Value:**
> "Our pilots are reporting [X]% reduction in implementation time and NPS scores of [X]."

---

### Month 3: Pilot Completions & Paid Conversions

**Week 9-10: Final Validation**
- [ ] Complete 60-day pilots
- [ ] Run final compliance audits
- [ ] Document complete ROI metrics
- [ ] Conduct exit NPS surveys

**Week 11-12: Conversion Discussions**
- [ ] Present subscription proposals
- [ ] Negotiate paid agreements
- [ ] Secure Letters of Intent from non-converters
- [ ] Finalize case studies

**Deliverables:**
- 3-5 successful pilot completions
- 1-2 paid customer agreements
- $100K+ in committed ARR (paid + LOIs)
- Final NPS scores (target: >50)
- 2+ published case studies
- 3+ reference customers confirmed

**Fundraising Value:**
> "We've converted [X] pilots to paid customers with $[X] in committed ARR and an NPS of [X]."

---

### Month 4: Begin Formal Fundraising

**Week 13-14: Prepare Materials**
- [ ] Update pitch deck with pilot metrics
- [ ] Finalize customer case studies
- [ ] Prepare data room
- [ ] Brief reference customers on investor calls

**Week 15-16: Active Fundraising**
- [ ] Execute warm introduction requests
- [ ] Schedule investor meetings
- [ ] Begin investor pitch meetings
- [ ] Track and iterate based on feedback

**Deliverables:**
- Updated pitch deck with traction data
- Complete data room
- Active investor pipeline
- First term sheet target: Week 20-24

## Risk Mitigation

### Pilot Risks & Mitigations

| Risk | Impact | Probability | Mitigation |
|------|--------|-------------|------------|
| **Pilot customer drops out** | Medium | Medium | Over-recruit (7-8 pilots for 5 completions) |
| **Technical issues during pilot** | High | Low | Dedicated success manager, rapid response |
| **Low NPS scores** | High | Low | Weekly check-ins, proactive issue resolution |
| **No conversions to paid** | High | Medium | LOIs as backup, multiple conversion paths |
| **Customers won't provide references** | Medium | Low | Reference commitment in pilot agreement |

### Contingency Plans

**If Fewer Than 3 Pilots Complete:**
- Extend timeline for remaining pilots
- Emphasize qualitative feedback and case studies
- Highlight lessons learned and product improvements

**If No Paid Conversions:**
- Focus on LOI commitments and pipeline value
- Emphasize NPS and customer satisfaction metrics
- Position as "pre-revenue with strong validation"

**If NPS < 50:**
- Identify and address specific issues
- Segment by customer type (some may be better fit)
- Focus on customers with highest scores for references
