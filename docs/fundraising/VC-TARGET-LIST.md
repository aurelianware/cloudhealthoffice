# Cloud Health Office - VC Target List & Research

**Prioritized Venture Capital Investment Targets**

---

## Executive Summary

This document identifies and prioritizes venture capital firms for Cloud Health Office's **$2M Seed Round**. Targets are selected based on investment thesis alignment with healthcare IT, SaaS, and regulatory-driven markets.

Cloud Health Office operates four product lines with distinct unit economics — **Public Tools** (free, funnel-top), **Transactional Services** (per-call APIs today, with self-serve onboarding and checkout in progress and recurring SaaS metrics expected as activation is completed), **Managed Data Services** (recurring healthcare-data subscriptions, high-margin recurring), and **Platform Engagement** (payer-scale PMPM relationships across Layer 1 — Compliance Accelerator, Layer 2 — Progressive Modernization, Layer 3 — Full CAPS Platform — multi-year contract economics). Different VC theses align differently to each product line: Enterprise SaaS firms find Transactional Services / Managed Data Services most legible; healthcare-vertical firms tend to underwrite Platform Engagement; firms that underwrite category consolidation evaluate the portfolio as a whole. Map each target below to which product lines best fit its thesis. Canonical breakdown: [POSITIONING.md](../POSITIONING.md) and [FINANCIAL-MODEL.md](../sales-materials/FINANCIAL-MODEL.md).

## Due Diligence Preparation by Investor Type

### Healthcare VCs Will Ask About:

- [ ] Payer market size and segmentation
- [ ] CMS-0057-F compliance details and timeline
- [ ] Competitive landscape and defensibility
- [ ] Healthcare domain expertise on team
- [ ] HIPAA compliance and security architecture
- [ ] Customer acquisition strategy for payers

### Enterprise SaaS VCs Will Ask About:

- [ ] Unit economics and cohort analysis
- [ ] Sales cycle and customer acquisition cost
- [ ] Net revenue retention and expansion opportunity
- [ ] Product-led growth potential
- [ ] Enterprise sales motion and pipeline
- [ ] Technical architecture and scalability

### Strategic VCs Will Ask About:

- [ ] Partnership and integration opportunities
- [ ] Strategic alignment with their portfolio/parent company
- [ ] Go-to-market collaboration potential
- [ ] Potential conflicts with existing investments
- [ ] Long-term vision and exit strategy
