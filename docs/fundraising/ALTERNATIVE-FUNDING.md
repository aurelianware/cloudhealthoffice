# Cloud Health Office - Alternative Funding Research

**Non-Traditional Funding Options Beyond Venture Capital**

---

## Overview

While venture capital is the primary funding strategy, this document evaluates alternative funding sources that may be appropriate for Cloud Health Office based on stage, market, and strategic objectives. These options can serve as complements to VC funding or alternatives if VC timing doesn't align.

Cloud Health Office (CHO) operates four product lines — **Public Tools**, **Transactional Services**, **Managed Data Services**, and **Platform Engagement** (payer-scale PMPM across Layer 1 — Compliance Accelerator, Layer 2 — Progressive Modernization, Layer 3 — Full CAPS Platform) — and several alternative funding instruments below align differently to each. Revenue-based financing fits Transactional Services / Managed Data Services (recurring revenue collateral); SBIR/STTR fits Public Tools and Platform Engagement R&D; strategic corporate investment fits Platform Engagement distribution. Match instrument to product line during evaluation. Canonical breakdown: [POSITIONING.md](../POSITIONING.md).

## 1. Revenue-Based Financing (RBF)

### Overview

Revenue-based financing provides capital in exchange for a percentage of future revenue until a predetermined multiple is repaid. No equity dilution, but requires existing revenue.

### Providers to Evaluate

#### Lighter Capital

**Profile:**
- Focus: Technology and SaaS companies
- Amount: $50K - $3M
- Terms: 5-8% of monthly revenue, 1.35-1.55x payback cap
- Timeline: 2-3 weeks to funding

**Requirements:**
- $15K+ MRR (minimum)
- 6+ months of revenue history
- SaaS or recurring revenue model
- US-based company

**Fit for Cloud Health Office:**
- ⬜ Currently: Requires revenue traction first
- ✅ Post-pilots: Could complement seed round

**Action Items:**
- [ ] Track as option post-revenue
- [ ] Research application requirements
- [ ] Model impact on cash flow

---

#### Clearco (formerly Clearbanc)

**Profile:**
- Focus: E-commerce and SaaS
- Amount: $10K - $10M
- Terms: 6-12% of revenue, flat fee
- Timeline: 24-48 hours (automated)

**Requirements:**
- Connected revenue data
- Consistent monthly revenue
- Digital business model

**Fit for Cloud Health Office:**
- ⬜ Currently: Requires revenue data
- ✅ Future: Fast option for growth capital

---

#### Pipe

**Profile:**
- Focus: Recurring revenue companies
- Amount: Based on ARR (up to 100% of ARR)
- Terms: Trade recurring revenue for upfront capital
- Timeline: Instant once connected

**Requirements:**
- Recurring revenue contracts
- Billing system integration
- Predictable revenue stream

**Fit for Cloud Health Office:**
- ⬜ Currently: Requires ARR
- ✅ Post-customers: Could accelerate without dilution

---

## 2. Venture Debt

### Overview

Venture debt provides loans to venture-backed companies, typically after an equity round. Offers capital efficiency by reducing dilution but requires equity cushion.

### When Venture Debt Makes Sense

- **After equity round**: Extends runway without additional dilution
- **Bridge financing**: Between funding rounds
- **Growth capital**: For specific initiatives (hiring, marketing)

### Providers to Evaluate

#### Silicon Valley Bank (SVB)

**Profile:**
- Focus: Technology and healthcare companies
- Amount: $1M - $25M+
- Terms: 3-4 year term, interest + warrants
- Requirements: VC-backed, post-Series A typical

**Fit for Cloud Health Office:**
- ⬜ Currently: Post-seed/Series A option
- ✅ Future: After equity round

---

#### Western Technology Investment (WTI)

**Profile:**
- Focus: Growth-stage tech companies
- Amount: $1M - $15M
- Terms: Flexible structures
- Requirements: Institutional backing

---

#### Horizon Technology Finance

**Profile:**
- Focus: Life sciences and healthcare technology
- Amount: $5M - $30M
- Healthcare focus could be good fit

---

### Venture Debt Assessment

| Criterion | Rating | Notes |
|-----------|--------|-------|
| **Current Eligibility** | ❌ | Requires prior equity |
| **Future Fit** | ✅ | Post-seed option |
| **Dilution Impact** | ✅ | Low (warrant coverage) |
| **Strategic Value** | 🔄 | Extends runway |
| **Recommendation** | **Consider post-seed round** | |

## 3. Government Grants (SBIR/STTR)

### Overview

The Small Business Innovation Research (SBIR) and Small Business Technology Transfer (STTR) programs provide non-dilutive funding for R&D with commercial potential. Healthcare IT is a target area.

### Relevant Programs

#### NIH SBIR/STTR

**Focus Areas Relevant to Cloud Health Office:**
- Healthcare interoperability
- Health IT innovation
- Data standards implementation
- Patient access technology

**Funding Phases:**
| Phase | Amount | Duration | Purpose |
|-------|--------|----------|---------|
| **Phase I** | $150K-$275K | 6-12 months | Feasibility |
| **Phase II** | $750K-$1.5M | 2 years | Development |
| **Phase IIB** | Up to $3M | Additional | Commercialization |

**Application Timeline:**
- 3-6 months from submission to award
- Multiple submission deadlines per year

**Fit for Cloud Health Office:**
- ✅ Healthcare IT focus aligns
- ✅ Non-dilutive capital
- ⚠️ Lengthy application process
- ⚠️ Reporting requirements

---

#### CMS Innovation Center

**Relevant Programs:**
- Health Care Innovation Awards
- State Innovation Model initiatives
- Interoperability pilots

**Fit:**
- ✅ Directly aligned with CMS compliance mission
- ⚠️ Competitive and specific requirements

---

**Focus:**
- Health IT certification
- Interoperability standards
- FHIR implementation

**Relevant Opportunities:**

## Decision Framework

### When to Pursue Alternative Funding

**Pursue Grants (SBIR) When:**
- ✅ Timeline allows 3-6 month application cycle
- ✅ R&D activities align with grant focus areas
- ✅ Team has grant writing capability
- ✅ Non-dilutive capital is priority

**Pursue RBF When:**
- ✅ $15K+ MRR established
- ✅ Want to extend runway without dilution
- ✅ VC timeline doesn't align

**Pursue Strategic Investment When:**
- ✅ Strategic value (distribution, validation) is critical
- ✅ Willing to accept potential conflicts/constraints
- ✅ VC round not coming together

**Pursue Crowdfunding When:**
- ⚠️ VC funding unsuccessful
- ⚠️ Strong community/mission narrative
- ⚠️ Willing to manage many small investors
