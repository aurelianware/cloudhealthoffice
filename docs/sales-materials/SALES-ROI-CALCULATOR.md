### ROI Calculation Formulas

**2. Platform Cost Savings**

```
Annual Platform Savings = Current EDI Spend - Cloud Health Office Subscription

Example:
  Current: $200,000/year
  Cloud Health Office: Contact sales for current pricing
  Savings: Significant reduction vs. legacy platforms
```

**2. Labor Efficiency Savings**

```
Hours Saved = (Queries/Year) × (Current Minutes - 5 min) / 60
Labor Savings = Hours Saved × Hourly Rate

Example:
  100,000 queries × (26 - 5) / 60 = 35,000 hours
  35,000 hours × $40 = $1,400,000/year
```

**3. Call Center Cost Reduction**

```
Calls Avoided = Queries × (Current Follow-up % - 10%)
Call Center Savings = Calls Avoided × Cost per Call

Example:
  100,000 × (30% - 10%) = 20,000 calls avoided
  20,000 × $10 = $200,000/year
```

**4. Compliance Risk Value**

```
Risk Mitigation Value = Probability of Non-Compliance × Average Penalty

Example:
  30% risk × $5,000,000 average impact = $1,500,000 risk avoided
```

**5. Total ROI**

```
Total Annual Benefit = Platform Savings + Labor Savings + Call Center Savings + Risk Value
ROI = (Total Annual Benefit / Annual Cloud Health Office Investment) × 100

Contact sales@cloudhealthoffice.com for a personalized ROI analysis.
```
