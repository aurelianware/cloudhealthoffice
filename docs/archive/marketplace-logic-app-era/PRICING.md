## ❓ Frequently Asked Questions

### Q: Can I change plans mid-month?
**A:** Yes. Upgrades take effect immediately. Downgrades take effect at next billing cycle.

### Q: What payment methods do you accept?
**A:** Credit card (Visa, Mastercard, Amex), ACH transfer (Enterprise), wire transfer (Custom).

### Q: Do you offer discounts for nonprofits or government?
**A:** Yes. 20% discount for 501(c)(3) nonprofits, government agencies, and educational institutions. Contact sales for verification.

### Q: What happens if I exceed transaction limits?
**A:** We'll send a warning at 80% usage. You can upgrade or pay overage fees ($0.05/transaction). No service interruption.

### Q: Can I cancel anytime?
**A:** Yes. No contracts. No penalties. Cancel via portal or email support@cloudhealthoffice.com.

### Q: Do you prorate refunds?
**A:** Yes. Cancel mid-month and receive prorated refund for unused days (minus transaction fees).

### Q: What's included in support?
**A:** All plans include email support, knowledge base, API documentation. Professional adds Slack channel. Enterprise adds dedicated CSM and phone support.
