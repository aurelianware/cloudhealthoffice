# Security Officer and Privacy Officer designation

**Status:** DRAFT. In effect when the CEO signs it. Keep the signed copy in the private compliance store for six years from when it was last in effect.
**Rule:** 45 CFR 164.308(a)(2) assigned security responsibility; 164.530(a) privacy official (applied to Aurelianware as a business associate through its BAAs).

A plan's security review asks for a named person on day one. One person may hold both roles in a company this size, with a backup who acts when they are unavailable for more than one business day.

## Responsibilities

**Security Officer**

- Owns the [security policies](POLICIES.md), the [risk analysis](RISK-ANALYSIS.md), the [contingency plan](CONTINGENCY-PLAN.md) and the [vendor BAA register](VENDOR-BAA-REGISTER.md).
- Approves every grant of access to the hosted environment, and runs the quarterly [access review](ACCESS-REVIEW.md).
- Leads every security incident under the [incident procedure](INCIDENT-RESPONSE.md), and acknowledges security alerts within one business day (within one hour for a plan whose contract requires it).
- Runs workforce [training](TRAINING-RECORD.md) and keeps the signed records.
- Reviews the policies and controls once a year and after any major change, and signs the yearly evaluation.
- Answers plan security questionnaires and audit requests, with the policies ready within three business days of a request.

**Privacy Officer**

- Owns how PHI is used and disclosed under each plan BAA, including minimum necessary.
- Runs the breach risk assessment (164.402) and sends the written notice to the plan.
- Handles a plan's requests for access, amendment or an accounting of disclosures, within the times its BAA sets.
- Keeps the list of each plan's BAA, its notice deadlines and its compliance contact.

## Authority

The Security Officer may, without further approval:

- remove or suspend anyone's access to the hosted environment;
- take a system offline, rotate credentials or block traffic to contain an incident;
- engage outside counsel or a forensic firm for a suspected breach, up to the insurance retention, and tell the CEO the same day.

## Changes

A new designation is signed whenever a role changes hands. Tell each plan's compliance contact within ten business days, or sooner if its BAA requires.
