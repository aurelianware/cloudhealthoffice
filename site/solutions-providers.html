Common Questions

Do I need to change my EHR?

No. Cloud Health Office sits alongside your existing EHR (Epic, Cerner, Athena, eClinicalWorks, etc.) via FHIR API integration. You keep your current clinical workflow.

What payers do you support?

Direct connections to UnitedHealthcare, Anthem, Aetna, Humana, Cigna, Blue Cross Blue Shield (major markets), Medicare, and Medicaid. Additional payers added monthly.

Is there a contract?

No. Solo & Small Group tiers are month-to-month. Group Practice tier requires 3-month minimum. Cancel anytime with 30-day notice.

How long does onboarding take?

Technical deployment: <1 hour. Payer credentialing (NPI enrollment, etc.): 2-4 weeks (standard industry timeline, not specific to us). You can start submitting test claims same day.

What about HIPAA compliance?

Architected for HIPAA from day one. Azure Private Link, encryption at rest/in transit, audit logging, BAA included. SOC 2 Type II certification in progress (Q3 2026).

Can I export my data?

Yes. Full data export via API or CSV at any time. No export fees. No restrictions. You own your data.
